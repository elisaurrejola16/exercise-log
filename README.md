# exercise-log
This repository contains the .html file for downloading a local app so that you can log your exercises done in the gym or at home. The app logs your height and weight, and can suggest a routine for you. It can also tell you when you should go up or down a weigth, by adding your reps. It is also available in Spanish or English.

[ENGLISH] To use this script as an app you must do the following, after opening this link:
https://elisaurrejola16.github.io/exercise-log/

## Phones:
### In Chrome from your Android device:
  Open the link, touch the menu (⋮), it should display the option 'Add to home screen' or 'Install app'.

### In Chrome from your iPhone:
  Open the link, click on 'Share', 'View More' → 'Add to Home Screen'.

### In Safari from your iPhone:
  Open the link, touch the menu (⋮), click on 'Share', 'View More' → 'Add to Home Screen'.

## Computers:
  From either Chrome or Edge, open the link, an install icon appears in the address bar, or menu → 'Install AlgaeTracker'.
